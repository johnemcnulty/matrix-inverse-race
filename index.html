      Rules

      • A row operation is Ri ← a·Ri + b·Rj + … using up to N−1 other rows (or none, to just scale), or a swap Ri ↔ Rj.
      • A turn holds 1 to N−1 operations, all read from the matrix as it stood at the start of the turn.
      • Each row may change once per turn, and operations can use a row's original values even if it changes this turn. A turn that would make the matrix lose its inverse is blocked.
      • When the left side is the identity, the game multiplies A by the right side to prove it's the inverse.
      • Some matrices have no inverse. Reduce until a row on the left is all zeros, then press No inverse. A wrong claim costs a turn.
